Entrants send exactly one word: the word their grandma would text about AI. A judge model compares words two at a time and the funniest, most grandma-like word wins.

Our entry is **beepboop** — the sound a grandma makes when she imitates a robot: warm, a little baffled, and unmistakably about machines. She doesn't know the jargon, so she reaches for the noise the thing makes instead.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```
